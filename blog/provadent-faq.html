What is ProvaDent and who is it for?

ProvaDent is a daily oral wellness supplement in capsule form, aimed at adults who want extra internal support for their teeth, gums and breath alongside normal brushing and flossing. It is not a substitute for dental care or for treating any dental condition.

What is actually inside ProvaDent?

The formula centers on calcium with vitamins D3, K2 and C, plus zinc, a probiotic-style blend intended for oral flora, cranberry extract and botanical breath-freshening compounds. Check the official label for the exact amounts of each.

How do I take ProvaDent each day?

Take 1 capsule daily, ideally with a meal and a full glass of water. Keep taking it consistently — like most oral wellness supplements, it is designed for regular daily use rather than occasional dosing.

Many users report noticing fresher-feeling breath within a few weeks, while changes in how teeth and gums feel tend to build over a longer stretch of consistent use. Give it at least a couple of months before judging results.
