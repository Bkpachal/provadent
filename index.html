What Are The Ingredients In ProvaDent?

ProvaDent is formulated with 8 named ingredients — including Probiotic Blend for Oral Flora, Calcium, Vitamin D3, Vitamin C, Zinc — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

  • 1

    Probiotic Blend for Oral Flora

    SUPPORTS A BALANCED MOUTH MICROBIOME

    A mix of beneficial bacterial strains intended to help populate the mouth with friendly microorganisms that compete with the plaque-forming kinds, supporting fresher breath and healthier gum tissue over time.

  • 2

    Calcium

    STRUCTURAL MINERAL FOR TEETH

    A well-known mineral that makes up much of the hard structure of teeth and bone; included to help supply the raw material teeth rely on for ongoing strength and remineralization support.

  • 3

    Vitamin D3

    HELPS THE BODY ABSORB AND USE CALCIUM

    Frequently paired with calcium because it assists absorption and utilization of the mineral, making it a logical companion in a formula aimed at maintaining tooth and gum integrity.

  • 4

    Vitamin C

    ANTIOXIDANT SUPPORT FOR GUM TISSUE

    A nutrient associated with collagen formation and normal gum tissue maintenance, helping protect delicate oral tissues from everyday oxidative stress.

  • 5

    Zinc

    MINERAL SUPPORT FOR ORAL FRESHNESS

    A trace mineral often used in oral care products because of its role in maintaining a healthy mouth environment and helping control the buildup that contributes to stale breath.

  • 6

    Cranberry Extract

    PLANT COMPOUND FOR MOUTH HYGIENE

    A fruit extract rich in natural polyphenols; it is commonly included in oral formulas because certain compounds in cranberries are thought to help discourage unwanted bacteria from sticking to tooth surfaces.

  • 7

    Vitamin K2

    DIRECTS CALCIUM WHERE IT BELONGS

    Often combined with D3, this vitamin is associated with helping the body route calcium toward teeth and bones rather than soft tissue, complementing the mineral side of the formula.

  • 8

    Botanical Breath-Freshening Extracts

    NATURAL FRESHNESS SUPPORT

    Plant-derived extracts such as mint-family compounds used to leave the mouth feeling clean and refreshed, addressing the day-to-day confidence side of oral wellness.
